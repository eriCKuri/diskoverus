Travel smart

Bottled or purified water only. 10% at restaurants; guides and drivers appreciate a daily tip.

Tanzania holds the largest share of the Serengeti-Mara ecosystem, so the Great Migration spends most of the year on its side of the border, not Kenya's. It's also one of the few countries where you can plausibly combine a snow-capped mountain summit, a Big Five safari, and a tropical island in a single two-week trip.

When to go

Long dry seasonJune–October

The classic safari window — thinning vegetation and scarce water concentrate wildlife, and the migration's river crossings peak July–September.

Short dry seasonDecember–February

Warm and mostly dry, with the migration's calving season on the southern Serengeti plains in January and February.

Long rainsMarch–May

The wettest, quietest stretch — some camps close, but rates drop sharply and the light for photography is excellent.

Zanzibar high seasonJune–October, December–February

The coast follows its own rhythm; these windows avoid the heaviest rain and give the calmest, clearest water.

Common questions

▾Is Tanzania safe to visit?

Tanzania is generally safe for tourists, with a well-established safari industry and low rates of violent crime against visitors. Standard precautions apply in Dar es Salaam and Zanzibar's Stone Town, where petty theft is a bigger risk than anything in the parks themselves.

▾How much does a Serengeti or Ngorongoro safari cost?

Serengeti National Park charges foreign non-residents roughly $70 to $85 per person per day, and Ngorongoro Conservation Area adds another $70 to $80 per person per day on top, plus separate vehicle and concession fees. That stacking is a big part of why a multi-park Tanzania safari reads as one of the pricier trips in Africa even before lodging. Rates are set in USD and revised yearly, so confirm the current figure with your operator before booking.

▾How many days do you need in Tanzania?

Most itineraries run 10-14 days to properly cover the northern circuit — Serengeti, Ngorongoro and Tarangire — plus a few days unwinding in Zanzibar afterward. A tighter 7-day trip still works if you drop the beach extension and focus on two or three parks.

▾Do I need a visa for Tanzania?

Most nationalities need a visa, available as an e-Visa in advance or on arrival for roughly $50 to $100 depending on your passport. US citizens pay a higher single-entry fee than many other nationalities, so check the exact cost for your country before you fly.

▾What's the best time for the wildebeest migration?

The migration moves through Tanzania's Serengeti roughly from December through July, with the dramatic Mara River crossings typically happening June through August as the herds push north toward Kenya. There's no single best month, since it depends on which stage of the migration you want to see.

▾Is Tanzania expensive?

Tanzania reads as a $$ to $$$ destination once park fees and mid-range lodges are factored in — similar to Kenya, and generally pricier than Uganda or Rwanda outside of gorilla permits. Budget group-camping safaris exist, but the classic lodge-based northern circuit is not a cheap trip.
