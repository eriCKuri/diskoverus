Istanbul straddling two continents, fairy-chimney valleys in Cappadocia, and a Mediterranean coastline layered with ancient ruins.

Travel smart

Bottled water recommended. 10–15% expected, often added as a service charge.

Istanbul is the only major city on Earth that sits on two continents, split by the Bosphorus strait between Europe and Asia. Cappadocia's landscape was shaped by volcanic ash eroding into cone-shaped 'fairy chimneys,' some carved into homes, churches, and entire underground cities more than a thousand years ago.

When to go

SpringApril–May

Mild temperatures, tulips blooming in Istanbul, and comfortable conditions for both the coast and Cappadocia before the summer heat and crowds arrive.

SummerJune–August

Hot, especially along the south coast, and the busiest and most expensive season — book balloon rides and coastal boats well ahead.

AutumnSeptember–October

Warm sea temperatures, thinner crowds, and generally the best weather window for hot air ballooning over Cappadocia.

WinterNovember–March

Cold and sometimes snowy inland, especially in Cappadocia, but the cheapest season and a genuinely beautiful one for snow-dusted fairy chimneys.

Common questions

▾Is Turkey safe to visit?

Istanbul, Cappadocia, and the Aegean and Mediterranean coasts see heavy tourism and are generally safe with normal precautions. Some southeastern border regions carry travel advisories — check current guidance for those specific areas, which are well outside the standard tourist circuit.

▾Do I need a visa for Turkey?

Most nationalities can apply for an e-visa online before arrival, typically around $50 and valid for 90 days. Some passport holders qualify for visa-free entry — check the requirement for your specific nationality before booking.

▾How many days do you need in Turkey?

Four to five days covers Istanbul well. Ten to fourteen days lets you add Cappadocia and a stretch of the Aegean or Mediterranean coast — the combination most first-time visitors aim for.

▾What's the best time to see the Cappadocia balloons?

Balloons fly nearly year-round weather permitting, but April through November offers the most reliable conditions, with September and October often cited as the sweet spot between summer heat and winter cancellations.

▾Is Istanbul really on two continents?

Yes — the Bosphorus strait splits the city between Europe and Asia, and locals commute across it daily by ferry, bridge, and metro. It's the only city of its size in the world to straddle two continents this way.

▾Is Turkey expensive?

It's one of the more affordable countries in this guide for US, UK, and EU travelers, thanks to a historically weak lira — food, transport, and guesthouses go a long way, though currency swings can shift this quickly.
