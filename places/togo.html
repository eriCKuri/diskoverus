Travel smart

Bottled or purified water only. Not expected; small tips are appreciated.

Togo's Akodessewa Fetish Market in Lomé is considered the largest Vodun fetish market in the world, supplying traditional healers and practitioners with ritual items in a genuinely active commercial and spiritual marketplace, not a tourist recreation. The Koutammakou region in northern Togo, a UNESCO World Heritage site, holds distinctive fortress-like Tamberma mud tower-houses (takienta), built for defense and still inhabited, reflecting an architectural tradition adapted specifically to the community's historic need for protection.

When to go

Main dry seasonNovember–February

The most comfortable window nationally, with lower humidity and clear skies for both the coast and interior.

Main wet seasonApril–July

The heaviest rains of the year in the south, though still workable with flexible planning.

Minor dry seasonAugust–September

A shorter, cooler dry spell between the two wet seasons in the south.

Minor wet seasonSeptember–October

A second, lighter rainy stretch before the main dry season returns in November.

Common questions

▾Do I need a visa for Togo?

US, UK, EU, Canadian, and Australian passport holders can get an e-visa online, allowing stays up to 90 days.

▾What is the Akodessewa Fetish Market?

Considered the largest Vodun fetish market in the world, supplying traditional healers and practitioners with genuine ritual items in an active commercial and spiritual marketplace in Lomé, not a staged tourist attraction.

▾What are the Tamberma tower-houses?

Distinctive fortress-like mud tower-houses (takienta) built by the Tamberma people in northern Togo's Koutammakou region, a UNESCO World Heritage site, designed for defense and still actively inhabited today.

▾How many days does Togo need?

7-9 days lets you combine Lomé's markets with a trip north to the Koutammakou region and Kpalimé's mountain scenery, given the country's narrow but elongated shape.

▾Is Togo safe for tourists?

Lomé and the southern circuit see regular, generally safe tourism; it's worth checking current advisories closely for northern border regions before finalizing an itinerary.
