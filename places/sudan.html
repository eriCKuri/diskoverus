Bottled or purified water only. 10% at restaurants; guides and drivers appreciate a daily tip.

Sudan holds more pyramids than Egypt, with over 200 built by the ancient Kingdom of Kush at sites like Meroë, a UNESCO World Heritage site reflecting a Nubian civilization that at times ruled over Egypt itself as its 25th dynasty of pharaohs. Since April 2023, Sudan has been engulfed in a devastating civil war between rival military factions that has displaced millions of people and created what the United Nations has repeatedly described as one of the world's worst humanitarian crises, and virtually all international tourism has ceased.

Common questions

▾Is it safe to travel to Sudan right now?

No — since April 2023, Sudan has been engulfed in a devastating civil war that has displaced millions of people and created what the United Nations has repeatedly described as one of the world's worst humanitarian crises. This page is a factual reference, not current travel guidance.

▾Why does Sudan have more pyramids than Egypt?

The ancient Kingdom of Kush built over 200 pyramids at sites like Meroë, a UNESCO World Heritage site, reflecting a powerful Nubian civilization that at times ruled over Egypt itself as its 25th dynasty of pharaohs.

▾Was Sudan a viable travel destination before the current war?

Yes — before April 2023, Sudan saw a small but growing niche of travelers specifically drawn to its remarkable, largely unvisited pyramid sites and Nile River heritage, historically offering a far less crowded alternative to Egypt's ancient sites.

▾What other ancient sites does Sudan hold?

Beyond Meroë, Sudan holds significant Nubian archaeological sites including Jebel Barkal and Old Dongola, reflecting millennia of continuous civilization along the Nile predating and rivaling ancient Egypt.

▾Why include Sudan on this site at all right now?

To provide accurate cultural and historical context for people researching or planning ahead, not as a suggestion to travel there under current conditions — always check official government advisories before making any decisions.

▾Is it safe to visit Sudan right now?

Most governments currently advise against all travel to Sudan given the ongoing conflict. This should be the primary consideration in any planning — check your government's latest advisory before making arrangements.
