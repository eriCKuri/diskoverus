Travel smart

Bottled or purified water only. Not required; small tips are appreciated.

India is less one destination than a continent's worth of them under one flag — a desert state, a tropical coast, and the world's highest mountain range are all within its borders, each with its own language, cuisine and rhythm. Most first-time visitors pick one region rather than attempting the whole country at once, and that's the right instinct.

When to go

WinterDecember–March

India's peak season for most regions — cool, dry and comfortable for sightseeing in Rajasthan, Delhi and the south.

SummerApril–June

Punishingly hot across most of the plains, though hill stations like Shimla and the Himalayan foothills offer relief.

MonsoonJuly–September

Heavy rain across most of the country — Kerala's backwaters and lush landscapes are at their most dramatic, but travel elsewhere gets harder.

Post-monsoonOctober–November

Green landscapes, clearing skies, and one of the best windows to combine sightseeing with more comfortable temperatures.

Common questions

▾Is India safe to visit?

India's major tourist circuits — the Golden Triangle of Delhi-Agra-Jaipur, Goa, Kerala and Rajasthan — are generally safe for visitors who take normal precautions around traffic, food and water, and solo female travelers in particular. Petty theft and scams around major tourist sites are a more common issue than violent crime.

▾How much does it cost to visit the Taj Mahal?

The foreigner entry ticket costs ₹1,300 (about $16), with an extra ₹200 if you want to enter the mausoleum's inner chamber where the actual tombs sit. That's a significant markup over the ticket price for Indian nationals, which runs closer to ₹50 — a gap that's common at major monuments across the country, not unique to the Taj Mahal.

▾How many days do you need in India?

12-14 days covers the classic Golden Triangle plus one region beyond it, like Rajasthan's forts or Kerala's backwaters, without feeling rushed. India rewards more time than that if you can spare it, since distances between regions are large and internal flights or long train rides eat into shorter trips.

▾Do I need a visa for India?

Most nationalities need a visa. The e-Tourist Visa costs about $25 for 30 days for most eligible countries including the US, UK, Canada and EU, with longer one-year and five-year options available for more. India has occasionally offered a discounted off-season e-Visa rate, so it's worth checking current pricing before you apply.

▾What's the best time to visit India?

October through March is the most comfortable window across most of the country, avoiding both the pre-monsoon heat and the monsoon rains themselves. Exactly when depends heavily on the region though, since Kerala's monsoon and the Himalayan trekking season run on entirely different calendars than Rajasthan's.

▾Is India expensive?

India is one of the most affordable major destinations in Asia for food, local transport and budget accommodation, even with the Taj Mahal's foreigner ticket price factored in. Costs rise quickly if you stick to international hotel chains or private drivers throughout, but a mid-range trip built around trains and local guesthouses is genuinely cheap by comparison.
