Travel smart

Tap water is safe to drink. Not customary.

Australia is the only country that is also an entire continent, home to more venomous species than anywhere on Earth despite being one of the safer countries to visit in practice. The Great Barrier Reef is the largest living structure on the planet and one of the only ones visible from space.

When to go

Southern summerDecember–February

Hot, dry, and the best beach weather in the south — also peak wildfire risk and the busiest, most expensive season.

Northern dry seasonMay–October

The only comfortable window to visit the tropical north — Kakadu, Darwin, and the Top End are humid and prone to flooding outside it.

Northern wet seasonNovember–April

Monsoon season across the north; dramatic storms and waterfalls, but some roads and parks close entirely.

Shoulder monthsMarch–May, September–November

Mild temperatures nationwide, making these the most comfortable months for a trip covering both the south and the outback.

Common questions

▾Is Australia safe to visit?

Yes — Australia is a safe, well-developed destination for tourists with low rates of violent crime. The practical risks travelers actually run into relate more to sun exposure, ocean currents and wildlife common sense than to crime.

▾How much does it cost to visit Uluru?

A park pass for Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park costs AUD 38 for three days or AUD 50 for an annual pass, with children under 18 free. That's a modest cost relative to the flights and accommodation needed to reach this remote part of the Outback, which make up the bulk of a Uluru trip's budget.

▾How many days do you need in Australia?

This depends heavily on scope given the country's size — a single-region trip (just the east coast, or just Uluru and the Red Centre) works in 10-14 days, while combining multiple regions means factoring in significant domestic flight time.

▾Do I need a visa for Australia?

Most visitors, including from the US, UK, Canada and EU, need an Electronic Travel Authority (ETA) or eVisitor visa, both quick, inexpensive online applications rather than a traditional visa process. Apply before you book flights, since approval can occasionally take longer than expected.

▾What's the best time to visit Australia?

This varies by region since Australia spans tropical and temperate zones — the south (Sydney, Melbourne) is best in its summer, December through February, while the tropical north (the Great Barrier Reef, Cairns) is best in its dry season, roughly May through October.

▾Is Australia expensive?

Australia reads as a $$$ destination — dining out, accommodation and car rental are on par with or above US prices in most cities. Costs come down noticeably if you self-cater and travel via campervan rather than relying on restaurants and hotels throughout.
