Travel smart

Bottled or purified water only. 10% at restaurants; guides and drivers appreciate a daily tip.

Somalia has the longest coastline on mainland Africa, and its ancient cave art at Laas Geel, discovered only in 2002, is estimated to be up to 5,000 years old, among the best-preserved and least-known rock art sites on the continent. Since the collapse of central government in 1991, Somalia has experienced decades of civil conflict and the ongoing threat of the al-Shabaab insurgency, and virtually every Western government maintains a Do Not Travel advisory for the entire country, including its capital, Mogadishu.

Long rains (Gu)April–June

The main wet season, historically bringing the most significant rainfall of the year.

Dry season (Hagaa)July–September

A cooler, drier period along the coast influenced by monsoon winds.

Short rains (Deyr)October–November

A secondary, lighter rainy season before the main dry season returns in December.

Common questions

▾Is it safe to travel to Somalia right now?

No — virtually every Western government maintains a Do Not Travel advisory for the entire country, including Mogadishu, due to terrorism, kidnapping, and ongoing armed conflict with al-Shabaab. This page is a factual reference, not current travel guidance.

▾Is Somaliland different from Somalia for travel purposes?

Somaliland is a self-declared independent region in Somalia's northwest with its own government and has seen a small amount of more accessible tourism, but it operates under separate arrangements and isn't covered by this page, which addresses Somalia as internationally recognized.

▾What is Laas Geel?

An ancient rock art site discovered only in 2002, estimated to be up to 5,000 years old, among the best-preserved and least-known prehistoric cave art sites on the African continent.

▾Why is Somalia's coastline significant?

It's the longest coastline on mainland Africa, historically central to Somalia's role in Indian Ocean and Red Sea trade for centuries before the country's prolonged instability.

▾Why include Somalia on this site at all right now?

To provide accurate cultural and historical context for people researching or planning ahead, not as a suggestion to travel there under current conditions — always check official government advisories before making any decisions.

▾Is it safe to visit Somalia right now?

Most governments currently advise against all travel to Somalia given the security situation, with some carving out narrower guidance for Somaliland in the north. Check your government's latest, region-specific advisory before making any plans.
