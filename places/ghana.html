Things to do in Ghana

Slave-trade fortresses that confront history directly, a warm and famously welcoming culture, and a coastline of surf towns and fishing villages increasingly on the radar for West Africa first-timers.

Travel smart

Bottled or purified water only. Not expected; small tips are appreciated.

Ghana's 'Year of Return' initiative in 2019 formally invited the African diaspora to reconnect with their ancestral homeland, and the country has since become a leading destination for Black heritage travel, anchored by the sobering Cape Coast and Elmina slave castles. It was also the first sub-Saharan African country to gain independence from colonial rule, in 1957, a milestone still central to national pride.

When to go

Major dry seasonNovember–March

The most popular window, with the Harmattan wind bringing dusty haze but reliably dry conditions for coastal and northern travel.

Major wet seasonApril–July

The heaviest rains of the year, especially in the south, though brief and often predictable afternoon downpours.

Minor dry seasonAugust–September

A shorter dry window between the two wet seasons, cooler and less humid in the south.

Minor wet seasonOctober

A second, lighter rainy stretch before the main dry season returns in November.

Common questions

▾Do I need a visa for Ghana?

Most nationalities, including US, UK, EU, Canadian, and Australian citizens, need an e-visa applied for online before arrival — build in a few days' processing time before your trip.

▾Is Ghana a good first stop for West Africa?

Yes, widely considered one of the more accessible entry points to the region — English-speaking, politically stable, with well-developed tourism infrastructure around Accra, Cape Coast, and Kumasi.

▾What should I know before visiting the slave castles?

Cape Coast and Elmina Castles are emotionally heavy but essential historical sites, especially significant for diaspora travelers; guided tours provide important context, and it's worth allowing time afterward to process the experience.

▾What vaccinations are recommended for Ghana?

Yellow fever vaccination is typically required for entry, and malaria prophylaxis is strongly recommended — consult a travel clinic well before departure for full, current guidance specific to your itinerary.

▾Is Ghanaian food very different from other West African cuisines?

It shares staples like jollof rice and fufu with neighboring countries but has its own distinct versions and specialties, including waakye and red-red, worth trying specifically in Ghana rather than assuming they're identical elsewhere.

▾How much does it cost to visit Cape Coast Castle?

Foreign adult entry runs roughly GHS 60-80 (about $4-5), covering a guided tour through the former slave-trading fort — a modest fee for one of West Africa's most historically significant sites. Ghanaian citizens and residents pay a separately reduced local rate.
