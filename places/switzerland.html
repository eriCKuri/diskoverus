Travel smart

Tap water is safe to drink. 5–10% is customary, often rounded to the nearest note.

Switzerland isn't in the EU but sits inside the Schengen area, and its rail network is often called the best in the world: trains, boats and cable cars are timed to connect within minutes of each other, even in remote valleys. Four national languages (German, French, Italian, Romansh) shift by region, sometimes within the same canton.

When to go

WinterDecember–February

Peak ski season in resorts like Zermatt, Verbier and St. Moritz, with Christmas markets in the lowland cities.

SpringMarch–May

Snow retreats from lower trails, waterfalls run high on snowmelt, and crowds and prices are both lower than summer.

SummerJune–August

The classic hiking season with full alpine trail access, also the busiest and most expensive months in the mountain resorts.

AutumnSeptember–November

Clear air, larch trees turning gold, and noticeably thinner crowds, often rated the best all-round hiking window.

Common questions

▾Is Switzerland safe to visit?

Yes. Switzerland is one of the safest countries in Europe for tourists, with very low crime rates. The main practical risks relate to mountain weather and altitude on high-elevation excursions like Jungfraujoch, rather than anything related to crime.

▾How much does it cost to visit Jungfraujoch?

A round-trip ticket to Jungfraujoch, 'Top of Europe', runs roughly CHF 200-260 depending on your starting station and the season, making it one of the priciest single excursions in the Alps. Discount rail passes (like the Swiss Travel Pass) reduce the price meaningfully if you're already planning to use Swiss trains elsewhere in your trip.

▾How many days do you need in Switzerland?

7-10 days covers a solid mix of cities and mountains, Zurich or Geneva plus a base in the Bernese Oberland or Zermatt region for mountain excursions. A shorter trip is workable if you focus on a single mountain base.

▾Do I need a visa for Switzerland?

US, UK, Canadian and most other Western passport holders can visit visa-free for up to 90 days within the Schengen Area, which Switzerland is part of despite not being an EU member. Longer stays or nationalities outside the visa-waiver list need a Schengen visa arranged in advance.

▾What's the best time to visit Switzerland?

June through September is the main hiking and mountain-excursion season, with the most trails and cable cars fully operational. December through March is the ski season, and the shoulder months between can see reduced services at higher elevations.

▾Is Switzerland expensive?

Switzerland is consistently one of the most expensive countries in Europe for food, lodging and transport, and mountain excursions like Jungfraujoch add a significant cost on top of general trip expenses. Buying a multi-day rail pass in advance is one of the more effective ways to reduce the total cost if you're moving between several regions.
