Things to do in Myanmar

Thousands of ancient Buddhist temples scattered across the Bagan plain, one of Southeast Asia's most striking archaeological landscapes, with tourism currently limited to established circuits given the country's ongoing civil war.

Travel smart

Bottled or purified water only. Not customary — rounding up is common.

The Bagan Archaeological Zone holds the remains of more than 2,000 Buddhist temples and pagodas built between the 9th and 13th centuries, making it one of the richest concentrations of ancient religious architecture anywhere in the world, comparable in scale and significance to Angkor. Myanmar has been in civil war since a February 2021 military coup, with armed resistance movements controlling significant territory outside the main cities; core established circuits including Yangon, Mandalay, Bagan, and Inle Lake continue to see regular tourist traffic, but most Western governments maintain a Do Not Travel advisory for the country overall.

When to go

Cool, dry seasonNovember–February

The most comfortable and popular window nationally for Bagan and Inle Lake, with lower humidity and clearer skies.

Hot seasonMarch–May

Rising temperatures across the country, particularly intense in central Myanmar around Bagan and Mandalay.

Monsoon seasonJune–October

Heavy rain nationwide, with some travel routes affected, though the established Yangon-Bagan-Mandalay-Inle circuit generally remains workable.

Transition periodOctober–November

Rains easing as the cool, dry season approaches, a good value window with fewer crowds.

Common questions

▾Is it safe to visit Myanmar right now?

Most Western governments maintain a Do Not Travel advisory for Myanmar overall given the ongoing civil war since the 2021 coup, though core established circuits including Yangon, Mandalay, Bagan, and Inle Lake continue to see regular, closely managed tourist traffic. It's essential to check current, area-specific conditions and travel only with an established operator.

▾Do I need a visa for Myanmar?

Most nationalities, including US, UK, EU, Canadian, and Australian citizens, need an e-visa applied for at least a week in advance; entry is only possible by air through Yangon or Mandalay, as land borders remain closed to tourists.

▾Why is Bagan considered so significant?

It holds the remains of more than 2,000 Buddhist temples and pagodas built between the 9th and 13th centuries, one of the richest concentrations of ancient religious architecture anywhere in the world.

▾Which areas of Myanmar should be avoided entirely?

Areas outside the established Yangon-Mandalay-Bagan-Inle Lake circuit generally see active conflict and shifting front lines, and should be avoided — stick to well-established routes and licensed operators who track current conditions closely.

▾How many days does Myanmar need?

9-11 days lets you combine Yangon, Bagan, Mandalay, and Inle Lake along the established circuit, given this is currently the only region most operators and governments consider workable for tourism.

▾How much does a hot air balloon ride over Bagan cost?

A 45-60 minute flight over Bagan's temples typically runs $300-420 per person, making it one of the pricier balloon rides in the world, well above similar flights in Turkey or Egypt. Flights only operate October through March outside of monsoon season, so factor that into when you visit if a balloon ride is a priority.
