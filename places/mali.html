Travel smart

Bottled or purified water only. Not expected; small tips are appreciated.

Timbuktu was one of the wealthiest and most important centers of Islamic scholarship and trans-Saharan gold and salt trade in the medieval world, and its manuscripts, some centuries old, represent one of the largest collections of West African written history anywhere on Earth. The Great Mosque of Djenné, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is the largest mudbrick building in the world, rebuilt annually by the entire community in a genuine, centuries-old collective tradition; however, since 2012 Mali has faced an escalating jihadist insurgency, and virtually all Western governments currently advise against all travel to the country.

Common questions

▾Is it safe to travel to Mali right now?

No — since 2012, Mali has faced an escalating jihadist insurgency, and virtually all Western governments currently advise against all travel to the entire country. This page is a factual reference, not current travel guidance.

▾Why is Timbuktu historically significant?

It was one of the wealthiest and most important centers of Islamic scholarship and trans-Saharan gold and salt trade in the medieval world, and its manuscripts represent one of the largest collections of West African written history on Earth.

▾What is the Great Mosque of Djenné?

A UNESCO World Heritage site and the largest mudbrick building in the world, rebuilt annually by the entire community in a genuine, centuries-old collective tradition.

▾Was Mali a popular travel destination before the insurgency?

Yes — before 2012, Mali was a well-established destination on the West African cultural travel circuit, particularly for Timbuktu, Djenné, and the Dogon Country's dramatic cliffside villages.

▾Why include Mali on this site at all right now?

To provide accurate cultural and historical context for people researching or planning ahead, not as a suggestion to travel there under current conditions — always check official government advisories before making any decisions.

▾Is it safe to visit Mali right now?

Most governments currently advise against all travel to Mali given the security situation across much of the country. Check your government's latest advisory before making any plans.
